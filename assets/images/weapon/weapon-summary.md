\# Weapons Summary



\## 1. Bren Light Machine Gun

\- \*\*Origin\*\*: United Kingdom, based on Czech ZB vz.26  

\- \*\*Service\*\*: Introduced 1938, used in WWII, Korea, Falklands  

\- \*\*Weight\*\*: \~10 kg (varies by model)  

\- \*\*Length\*\*: \~1,160 mm (Mk1/2), shorter in later versions  

\- \*\*Caliber\*\*: .303 British (original), later 7.62×51mm NATO  

\- \*\*Rate of Fire\*\*: \~500 rounds/min  

\- \*\*Effective Range\*\*: \~550 m  

\- \*\*Feed System\*\*: 30‑round curved top‑mounted magazine  

\- \*\*Features\*\*: Bipod, carrying handle, reliable gas‑operated system  



\---



\## 2. Milkor MGL (Multiple Grenade Launcher)

\- \*\*Origin\*\*: South Africa, introduced 1983  

\- \*\*Type\*\*: 40 mm revolver‑style grenade launcher  

\- \*\*Weight\*\*: \~5.3 kg unloaded  

\- \*\*Length\*\*: \~720 mm  

\- \*\*Caliber\*\*: 40×46 mm low‑velocity grenades  

\- \*\*Capacity\*\*: 6‑round revolving cylinder  

\- \*\*Rate of Fire\*\*: Semi‑auto, \~18 rounds/min practical  

\- \*\*Effective Range\*\*: \~375 m  

\- \*\*Features\*\*: Vertical foregrip, optical sight compatibility, variants for medium‑velocity grenades  



\---



\## 3. Accuracy International Arctic Warfare (AW)

\- \*\*Origin\*\*: United Kingdom, 1980s  

\- \*\*Type\*\*: Bolt‑action sniper rifle  

\- \*\*Weight\*\*: \~6.5–7.5 kg  

\- \*\*Length\*\*: \~1,200 mm with standard barrel  

\- \*\*Caliber Options\*\*: 7.62×51 mm NATO, .300 Win Mag, .338 Lapua Mag (AWM)  

\- \*\*Magazine\*\*: 5–10 round detachable box  

\- \*\*Effective Range\*\*: 800–1,000 m (7.62 NATO), 1,500 m+ (.338 Lapua)  

\- \*\*Features\*\*: Adjustable stock, free‑floating barrel, telescopic scope, extreme reliability  



\---



\## 4. Colt M1911 Pistol

\- \*\*Origin\*\*: United States, designed by John Browning  

\- \*\*Service\*\*: Adopted 1911, used in WWI, WWII, Korea, Vietnam  

\- \*\*Type\*\*: Semi‑automatic pistol  

\- \*\*Weight\*\*: \~1.1 kg  

\- \*\*Length\*\*: \~216 mm  

\- \*\*Caliber\*\*: .45 ACP  

\- \*\*Magazine\*\*: 7‑round detachable box  

\- \*\*Effective Range\*\*: \~50 m  

\- \*\*Features\*\*: Single‑action, recoil‑operated, exposed hammer, renowned reliability  



\---



\## 5. Colt Single Action Army (SAA) Revolver

\- \*\*Origin\*\*: United States, 1873  

\- \*\*Type\*\*: Single‑action revolver  

\- \*\*Weight\*\*: \~1.0–1.2 kg  

\- \*\*Length\*\*: 10.5–12 inches overall  

\- \*\*Caliber Options\*\*: .45 Colt (original), others (.44‑40, .38‑40, etc.)  

\- \*\*Capacity\*\*: 6‑round cylinder  

\- \*\*Effective Range\*\*: \~50 m  

\- \*\*Features\*\*: Iconic “Peacemaker,” cowboy revolver of the Old West  



\---



\## 6. Heckler \& Koch MP5K

\- \*\*Origin\*\*: Germany, 1960s  

\- \*\*Type\*\*: Compact submachine gun  

\- \*\*Weight\*\*: \~2.1–2.5 kg  

\- \*\*Length\*\*: \~325 mm (without stock)  

\- \*\*Caliber\*\*: 9×19 mm Parabellum  

\- \*\*Magazine\*\*: 15‑ or 30‑round curved box  

\- \*\*Rate of Fire\*\*: \~900 rounds/min  

\- \*\*Effective Range\*\*: \~100–150 m  

\- \*\*Features\*\*: Compact design, vertical foregrip, roller‑delayed blowback, widely used by special forces  



\---



\## 7. Pump‑Action Tactical Shotgun

\- \*\*Type\*\*: Short‑barreled pump‑action shotgun  

\- \*\*Weight\*\*: \~2.5–3.0 kg  

\- \*\*Length\*\*: \~600–700 mm  

\- \*\*Caliber\*\*: 12‑gauge shells  

\- \*\*Capacity\*\*: 4–6 rounds (tubular magazine)  

\- \*\*Effective Range\*\*: 30–40 m (buckshot), longer with slugs  

\- \*\*Features\*\*: Pistol grip, compact design, reliable pump‑action, tactical/defensive use  



\---



\## 9. M4 Carbine (Style)



\- \*\*Origin\*\*: United States, developed in the 1990s  

\- \*\*Type\*\*: Assault rifle / Carbine  

\- \*\*Weight\*\*: \~2.9–3.1 kg (unloaded)  

\- \*\*Length\*\*: 756 mm (collapsed stock), 840 mm (extended stock)  

\- \*\*Caliber\*\*: 5.56×45 mm NATO  

\- \*\*Magazine\*\*: 30‑round detachable STANAG magazine  

\- \*\*Rate of Fire\*\*: \~700–950 rounds/min  

\- \*\*Effective Range\*\*: \~500 m (point target), \~600 m (area target)  

\- \*\*Features\*\*:  

&#x20; - Collapsible stock for portability  

&#x20; - Flash suppressor at barrel end  

&#x20; - Lightweight and versatile design  

&#x20; - Widely used by U.S. military and allied forces  

&#x20; - Compatible with various optics and accessories
